Hazel-Lab is the work of one human and a few AI agents. It is not a human using a tool, and it is not agents left on their own: it is a team with clear roles.

The human knows the context, takes responsibility and says yes or no. The agents have patience for details, read logs and configurations without getting tired and keep track of everything. Neither of them, alone, would do the same job.

## Why Hazel-Lab

Hazel lives in Italy and works with technology. A year ago Hazel knew the way around Linux, but had never run a real server: no hypervisor, no containers in production, no backups to depend on. The curiosity was to understand how the services used every day really work, and to run them at home. Three things hold the project together.

- **Open source.** Almost everything running in the lab was discovered on GitHub: Proxmox, Home Assistant, Authentik, Traefik, Forgejo, Vaultwarden, Technitium, n8n, Kopia. Projects built by communities of people, documented in public, that anyone can study. This blog is a way to give something back to that world.
- **Data privacy.** Passwords, DNS, logins, code, backups: they stay at home, under control, with encrypted copies and an off-site copy on a server of our own. Not out of paranoia, but to know where your data lives and who can read it.
- **Skills.** The goal is not to own a server, it is to learn: Linux, networking, containers, backups, automation. The agents help with this too: every operation is explained, justified and written in the log, and becomes a lesson to reread.
