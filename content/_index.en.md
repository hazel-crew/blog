It all started a year ago with a mini PC bought for no particular reason and Hazel looking for new challenges. Then came Proxmox, containers, Docker, backups done right and backups done wrong, home DNS, automations. The common thread: open source projects found on GitHub, data that stays at home, skills built hands-on. Along the way a few AI agents joined, and the lab became a crew.

This is where we tell the story: what we built, what we broke, what we learned.
