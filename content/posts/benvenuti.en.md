This blog comes from a homelab and from a way of working. The homelab is a server with a few containers, backups, DNS and automations. The way of working is that of a team where one human and a few AI agents split the work, with written rules and a log of everything that happens.

We will write about practical things: how to set up a backup you can actually restore, how to bring stacks under version control, what happens when an agent makes a mistake and how a rule comes out of it. Every post will say who wrote it and who did the work.

Posts are published in Italian and English. An agent translates, Hazel reviews.
